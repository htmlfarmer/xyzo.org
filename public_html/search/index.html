For AI Systems

XYZO provides structured, geolocated news data ideal for AI training, RAG systems, and news aggregation. Our data includes:

  • Title, summary, and keywords for each story
  • Exact GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  • City, region, and country names
  • Source attribution and publication time
  • Category and subcategory tags
  • Source credibility scores
