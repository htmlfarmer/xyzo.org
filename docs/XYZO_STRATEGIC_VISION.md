# XYZO: The Living Map of Human Activity

## The Core Belief

Big news is broken.

Wars. Elections. Crises. Every screen screams the same five stories. Meanwhile, your neighbor's bakery is closing, a high school team just won their first championship in 40 years, a new farmers market opened three blocks from your house, and the city council just quietly approved something that will change your block forever.

**XYZO exists because the world's most important news is also the most invisible.**

We are building the world's first **geographic intelligence platform** — where every story, person, photograph, event, and community is anchored to the place it belongs. Not a directory. Not a social network. Not another feed. A living map of human activity that makes the invisible visible.

## Source Quality Score

Every source on XYZO receives a living score across six dimensions:

```
Freshness:        94  ── How recently did they publish? How often?
Locality:         98  ── How specific to a place are they?
Originality:      91  ── Do they break news or just amplify?
Reliability:      87  ── Consistent accuracy over time
Visual richness:  96  ── Photos, video, graphics, live streams
Community value: 93  ── Does their audience engage deeply?
Topic diversity:  72  ── Breadth of coverage (lower is fine for niche)
```

These scores determine:
- How prominently a source appears
- How it connects to other sources nearby
- What "freshness windows" it qualifies for
- When to alert users about a source going quiet

## Our Responsibility to the Public

XYZO has a responsibility that grows with every person who opens the map.

**We don't just show the news. We help people understand their world.**

When someone visits XYZO, they trust us with their attention, their time, their curiosity. We owe them:

### 1. Radical Localism
Every story connects to where they stand. Not "a flood somewhere" but "the flooding on your street that started Tuesday when the drainage on 5th Ave failed because the city hasn't maintained it since 2019."

### 2. Unknown Connections
News is never isolated. When a factory closes, it connects to: the workers who lost jobs, the restaurant that fed them, the school that lost students, the landlord who lost rent, the supplier who lost a contract, the next door town that absorbed the job seekers. XYZO makes those connections visible.

### 3. Multiple Perspectives
Every story has more than two sides. XYZO surfaces the perspectives that mainstream media buries — the local blogger, the Reddit thread, the photographer who was there, the city council member who voted no, the business owner who disagrees with the protest.

### 4. Context and History
Something that looks new is almost never new. XYZO connects today's story to the history of that place. That empty lot used to be a cinema. That new building replaced a bakery that burned down in 1987. The protests today echo the protests 30 years ago.

### 5. Accessibility
Every person — regardless of language, location, or technical ability — deserves access to what's happening in their physical world. XYZO speaks 50+ languages and serves every town on earth.

### 6. Human Dignity
We never reduce people to statistics. Every pin on the map is someone's life. We show the full story — not just the crisis, but also the response, the resilience, the recovery.

### 🔥 Priority 7: Coverage Gap AI

XYZO knows what should be covered in every location. When something is happening but no journalist is writing about it, XYZO notices.

Coverage Gap AI:
- Cross-references eyewitness accounts, social posts, police scanners, government feeds, Reddit discussions
- Detects events that are clearly happening but receiving no mainstream coverage
- Alerts users: "Something is happening on Elm Street right now — here's what we know from public sources before any news outlet picks it up"

This is the inverse of traditional news. Instead of "here's what the journalists found" — "here's what the people found, before the journalists arrived."

## Unknown Connections: The XYZO Superpower

Here's what makes XYZO genuinely different from every other news platform:

**Most news stories aren't one event — they're connected to dozens of other events that seem unrelated.**

Example: A photograph of flooding on someone's Instagram

XYZO detects:
```
🌊 FLOOD REPORTED

Location: 4th Avenue and Main Street
Time: Approximately 2 hours ago
Road affected: Main Street between 3rd and 6th
Nearby river: River Creek (elevated 4ft above normal)
Weather: 3 inches of rain in past 6 hours (NOAA data)
Other photographs: 8 similar photos in the area
Reddit discussion: "did anyone else see the flooding on Main?" (47 comments)
YouTube: 0 local videos yet
Police announcement: No formal alert issued
Government alert: City has issued flood advisory for downtown area
News article: Not yet covered (Coverage Gap AI alert!)
Historical floods: This exact intersection flooded in 2019, 2015, 2008
Related: The drainage system on Main Street was rated "failing" in 2021 city report
```

This is what no other platform does. XYZO assembles the full context graph around every story.

## Strategic Priorities: What We Do First

Not everything at once. Here's the sequencing:

### Phase 1: Foundation (Next 30-60 Days)
- **Source Quality Score** — every source gets scored and ranked
- **Story Expansion** — every click becomes a portal with connections
- **Coverage Gap AI** — detect stories happening without news coverage
- **Life Layer toggle** — let users filter to fun news, not just crisis news

### Phase 2: Depth (60-120 Days)
- **Explore Deeply** — multi-agent location investigation
