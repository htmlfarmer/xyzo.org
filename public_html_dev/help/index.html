🐛 Bug Fixes & Changelog

Fixed Search Clear Restores All Markers
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: After searching for something (e.g., "health"), clicking the X to clear would keep showing only health-related markers instead of restoring all markers from the viewport.
Root Cause: The clearSearch() function was clearing event markers but keeping the existing pinMarkers (which contained only search results). It wasn't re-fetching viewport pins.
Fix: Modified clearSearch() to call pinMarkers.clear() before fetchPinsForViewport(), ensuring fresh viewport pins are loaded when search is cleared.
Fixed Category Filter Case Sensitivity Bug
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: Category filters stored in localStorage could have mixed case (e.g., "Tech"), causing the filter matching to fail since marker categories are always lowercased. This resulted in markers not showing even when their category was filtered.
Root Cause: toggleCategoryFilter() stored keys as-is, and loadCategoryFilters() didn't validate against known legend keys.
Fix: Modified toggleCategoryFilter() to always store lowercased keys. Modified loadCategoryFilters() to validate against known LEGEND_GROUPS keys and reject unknown categories. Added CATEGORY_ROLLUPS constant to define parent-to-subcategory mappings.
Fixed Missing CATEGORY_ROLLUPS Definition
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: JavaScript error: ReferenceError: CATEGORY_ROLLUPS is not defined when trying to filter markers by category.
Root Cause: The CATEGORY_ROLLUPS constant was referenced in syncLayers() but never defined.
Fix: Added the CATEGORY_ROLLUPS constant at the top of app.js, mapping parent buckets (like "technology") to their subcategories (["ai", "cybersecurity", "gaming", "telecom"]).
Fixed Popup Too Large on Mobile
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: Clicking markers on mobile devices showed popups that were too large for the screen.
Fix: Added CSS media queries for .popup-inner at 600px and 480px breakpoints to constrain popup width to calc(100vw - 48px) and limit max-height. Also added mobile-specific styles for feedback dialog.
Fixed Missing /api/pins/map Route
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: Map pins not loading - console showed "pins/map fetch failed 400". The frontend calls /api/pins/map for world/continent view but this route was missing from the API.
Fix: Added the /api/pins/map endpoint to api/src/routes/pins.ts - a lightweight endpoint that returns only the fields needed for canvas rendering.
Fixed Feedback Dialog Missing CSS
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: The feedback dialog (triggered when clicking "Fix" on a story) had no CSS styling defined.
Fix: Added complete CSS for .feedback-dialog and related classes to the stylesheet, matching the visual language of the quick-flag dialog.
Fixed Coverage & Discover Buttons Not Working
Date: 2026-09-04
Issue: Clicking the "Coverage" button (search cities and discover news coverage) did nothing. The "Discover" button also had no functionality.
Root Cause: Neither coverage-btn nor discover-btn had any event listeners attached in app.js. The buttons were defined in index.html but not wired up.
Fix: Added event handlers for both buttons. The Coverage button now opens the Coverage panel with city search functionality (via /api/locations/search), allowing users to fly to cities and discover news. The Discover button opens the Explore Region panel.
📝 Note: Bug reports can also be submitted using the form above. For technical details and test cases, see XYZO Test Suite.

XYZO — Map the World in Real Time

We believe the world's information belongs on a map.
Every story, every city, every moment — visualized in one radical new way.

Our Mission

XYZO is building the most comprehensive, real-time map of global news ever created. We scrape, analyze, and geolocate stories from thousands of sources across every continent, then plot them on an interactive map so you can see what's happening anywhere on Earth — right now.

Our goal is to help people understand the world differently. Not through feeds or feeds or algorithmic feeds — but through place. Through geography. Through seeing patterns emerge on a map that you simply cannot see any other way.

We're committed to free and open information. We only work with news sources that are 100% free and open — no paywalls, no subscription gates. We read every robots.txt, respect every user agreement, and partner only with publishers who believe information should be accessible to everyone.

Emergency Shutdown — The Community Brake 🚨

Here's something you won't find on most websites: you can shut us down.

If enough people file "significant issue" complaints through the help panel, XYZO automatically goes into maintenance mode. Think of it like Wikipedia's emergency bot shutdown — if the community says something is very wrong, we listen.

How it works:

  • Go to the help panel (click ❓ on the map) and scroll to "Report a Serious Issue"
  • Describe what's broken or wrong — be specific
  • If complaints from different users exceed 10% of active users in 24 hours, the site automatically enters maintenance mode
  • An administrator reviews the complaints and resets the system

This is our promise: 90% of the world needs to be happy with XYZO, or it goes offline. We'd rather shut down and fix things than keep running when something is seriously wrong.

#WIKIPEDIA inspired us on this one. Thanks, Wikipedia! 🎉

Read the World in Any Language 🌐

XYZO ingests news from every continent, in every language. We don't just read English — we read Spanish from Venezuela, Bahasa from Indonesia, Hindi from India, Arabic from the Middle East, and dozens more.

Try it: Search for a topic you care about, then zoom into a different country. The news looks different when you see it through local eyes.

Some examples of what you'll find when you read beyond your home language:

  • 🇮🇩 Lake Maracaibo, Venezuela — environmental stories about the world's largest lake and its communities
  • 🇦🇺 Australia — bushfire season coverage, reef conservation, and Outback stories
  • 🇮🇳 India — local politics, tech hubs, and cultural events that never make Western headlines
  • 🇳🇬 Nigeria — Nollywood, tech startups, and the vibrant Lagos scene
  • 🇰🇷 South Korea — K-culture, tech innovation, and inter-Korean relations

The world is bigger than your newsfeed. XYZO helps you see it.

How to Contribute

There are many ways to be part of XYZO:

  • Report sources — If you know of a great free news source we should be tracking, use the "Report a bad location" or "Request coverage" button on the map to let us know.
  • Correct locations — Spot a story in the wrong place? Every pin has a "Fix location" button. Your corrections help train our geolocation AI.
  • Spread the word — Share XYZO with friends, colleagues, researchers, and anyone who wants a better way to understand global news.
  • Build with us — We're looking for collaborators in AI/ML, data journalism, open-source mapping, and visualization. If you have ideas for new features, data sources, or ways to make XYZO more powerful, we want to hear from you.
  • Donate — XYZO runs on passion and purpose. If you believe in what we're building, every contribution helps keep the servers running and the news flowing.

Legal & Ethics

Content & Copyright

All news articles and content displayed on XYZO belong to their respective publishers. We do not claim ownership of any content. We aggregate and geolocate publicly available RSS feeds and display them on a map for informational purposes. If you are a publisher and would like your content removed, please contact us and we will promptly comply.

robots.txt & User Agents

We strictly honor every robots.txt directive. Our crawlers identify themselves with a clear User-Agent string and we never circumvent access restrictions. We respect all site terms of service and user agreements.

Free & Open Sources Only

XYZO only indexes news sources that are genuinely free and accessible to everyone — no paywalls, no subscription requirements, no registration gates. We do not intentionally index content behind paywalls or that violates publisher terms.

Accuracy & Corrections

News geolocation is hard. We use AI to extract locations from articles, but mistakes happen. If you see a story in the wrong place, please use the "Fix location" button to report it — your correction makes XYZO better for everyone.

Privacy

XYZO stores anonymous usage analytics (viewport moves, searches, clicks) to improve the service. We do not track or store personally identifiable information. We use session cookies for map state and basic analytics only.
